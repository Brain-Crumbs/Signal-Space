# Current GROSS research

The authoritative program is
[papers/gross-operator-program-v0.2.md](papers/gross-operator-program-v0.2.md),
interpreted alongside the current registered models, protocols, locked configs
and verified evidence. Follow the [Tests 1–11 ledger](../docs/research/gross-progress.md)
for current status rather than older report conclusions.

`experiments/` preserves GROSS canonical evidence and historical reader exports
at their original paths. `catalog.json` is the current GROSS discovery index.
`archive-manifest.json` records content hashes; regenerate it only when
intentionally adding or updating research records, never to conceal tampering.
New large figures and raw evidence should be delivered as run artifacts.

Older charged/knot and Paper I research is archived under
`../archive/pre-gross/research/` and is not an active model specification.
